PREPRINTS · 2026

Refining Hardness after ETH: Task-Relevant Distinctions and the Cost of Exact Counting

2026 · preprint · Lean 4 development, 709 audited declarations

Takes the reported subexponential counting algorithms as stated and asks what stays hard: a fully formalized finite analysis of the coordinate-swap construction behind one of them, which bounds the enumeration it completes at eventually 2^(n^(1−ε)) under the stated row and dimension budgets.

What a Mutation Regime Can Know About a Function

2026 · preprint · 38 Lean theorems, a checker and an experiment

Characterizes what a full mutation score against output mutation can establish about a program, for every program and every suite: a regime is sufficient for a target class iff every target footprint is a union of the regime’s footprints, with the knowability boundary that follows for Descartes-style constant mutants.
